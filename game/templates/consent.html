Participant Information Sheet & Consent Form

Study Title: Training game for phishing_game in enterprise

Researcher: 3060584G@student.gla.ac.uk

Ethics Contact: Prof Matthew Chalmers, SoCS Ethics Chair

1. Study Aim

This research aims to evaluate a two-way anti-phishing training platform. The system supports standard phishing identification training and a creative module where participants design deceptive phishing emails. We measure users’ ability to recognise social engineering attack techniques through behavioural logs.

2. What you will do during the study

After registration, you first need to complete the **T0 baseline test** from your profile page (this test can only be completed once). Then you will complete staged training (L1 basic phase, L2 role‑based simulation phase, unlockable L3 adversarial creation phase). After finishing all training tasks, you will take the **T1 post‑training test**, which can also only be taken once. The whole procedure will take approximately 12‑15 minutes.

The system records your behaviours: mark emails as legitimate/suspicious, delete emails, report phishing emails, and click fake hyperlinks inside simulated emails.

3. Data collected & Anonymisation Rule

You will create a self-defined username solely for system login purposes, and this login name will not be used in any research dataset. Upon successful registration, the system will automatically assign you a unique random anonymous participant ID (a string of random characters) for all research data records. No real name, phone, email or other personally identifiable information will be collected, stored or linked to your experimental behavioural logs at any stage.

The mapping between your login username and random anonymous ID will be permanently erased once all data collection for this research finishes. All experimental data will be stored on a password-protected local device and fully deleted within 12 months after dissertation submission.

4. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw

Your participation is fully voluntary. You may close the browser and withdraw at any time without penalty. All your recorded behavioural data will be permanently deleted upon your request. If you exit before finishing training, you cannot receive full debrief information about the experiment’s purpose.

This is an evaluation of the training system, not a test of your personal ability.

5. Confidentiality & GDPR Compliance

No identifiable personal data will be collected or shared publicly. Only aggregated anonymised results labelled by random anonymous ID may be used in the dissertation, with no way to trace back to any single participant.

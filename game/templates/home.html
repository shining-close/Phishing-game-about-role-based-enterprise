This platform provides role‑isolated phishing simulation scenarios. Each employee can access training emails corresponding to their department: Human Resources, Finance, IT.

Workflow: Complete one‑time T0 Baseline Test in Personal Center → Repeatable Level1 Basic Test (finish L1 to unlock L2). Level 2 rules: Correctly identify emails +6 points per mail, misjudge ‑4 points, click phishing link ‑6 points per mail, click right link +4 points per mail; max 30 points per session, accumulate total points to unlock L3. → Level3 Email Creation: Four‑dimension automatic scoring (0‑100): role‑matching(0‑30), social‑engineering(0‑30), camouflage(0‑25), flaw‑hiding(0‑15). High‑score drafts (≥70, Good/Excellent) will be added to L2 training pool for other users. → Complete one‑time T1 Post‑Training Test → Fill post‑experiment questionnaire.

Note: Focus on the content and urls. T0 baseline test, T1 post‑training test and post‑experiment questionnaire can only be submitted once per user. If you have any questions, please contact the us: 3060584G@student.gla.ac.uk.

Debriefing Statement

Study Purpose

Thank you very much for completing our online email security experiment. This research aims to investigate how employees from different departments identify and respond to phishing emails under varying training difficulty levels. During the task, you were asked to judge a series of simulated legitimate and phishing emails, and your operational behaviours (marking, reporting, clicking links) were recorded to measure your phishing detection accuracy.

Prior to your participation, we did not fully disclose the core research questions to avoid biased decision-making during the test. Our primary hypothesis is that progressive interactive training can significantly improve participants’ ability to recognise malicious phishing communications and reduce risky click behaviours. All T0 baseline tests, L2 training sessions and T1 post-training assessments are designed to compare security awareness before and after training intervention.

What Data We Collected

We only collect anonymous behavioural data linked to your system account, with no real personal identifiers permanently stored in the database. Recorded information includes your email operation logs, judgement results, test scores, training session records and self-reported confidence ratings during T0 and T1 tests.

Your real full name submitted on the informed consent page is only temporarily stored in browser session data and will be completely deleted immediately after account registration; it will never be written into the formal research dataset or shared with any third party. All analytical results in the final dissertation will be aggregated statistics without individual user traceability.

Data Storage & Destruction

All experimental data will be securely stored on a local offline MySQL database for a maximum of 12 months after the completion of this research project. Once the master’s dissertation is submitted and assessed, all raw user records will be permanently erased from the database with no backup copies retained.

You reserve the full right to withdraw your experimental data from this research at any time without any negative consequences. If you wish to remove all your behavioural records, please contact the researcher via the email address provided below.
